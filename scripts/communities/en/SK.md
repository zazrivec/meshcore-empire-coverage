## Slovakia

- [mesh.om3kff.sk](https://mesh.om3kff.sk) — main coordination hub: frequencies, hardware recommendations, how-tos, FAQ, contacts.
- [meshcore.estranky.sk](https://meshcore.estranky.sk/) — "MeshCore Network in Slovakia".
- [cq.sk — MeshCore IoT activities in Slovakia](https://cq.sk/en/meshcore-iot-activities-in-slovakia/) — European Hamradio portal.
- [ARES SZR — LoRa MESH for emergency communication](https://aresom.wordpress.com/lora-mesh-siete-pre-tiesnovu-komunikaciu-siete-meshtastic-a-meshcore-na-slovensku/)
- [om3kff.sk (tag: MeshCore)](https://om3kff.sk/tag/meshcore/?lang=sk) — blog and news, including Bratislava's switch to the 869.618MHz/SF8/BW62.5/CR8 preset.

Community centred around OM3KFF/OM5M (Bratislava), with regular meetups **every Thursday at 17:30** at Rádioklub Omega, Staré Grunty 53, Mlynská dolina.
